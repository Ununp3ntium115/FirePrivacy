Consumer source preview · iPhone & iPad

Your activity.
A clearer view.

Explore the App Privacy Report you choose to import. Understand recorded domain contacts and sensor access in a calm, private workspace on your device.

No account. No ads. Optional networking uses explicit consent and data-flow controls.

01 · IMPORT

Start with your report.

Export an App Privacy Report from Settings to Files, then choose it in Fire Privacy. A clearly labeled synthetic example lets you explore first.

02 · EXPLORE

Read the evidence.

Browse recorded app activity, domain contacts, and sensor access. Each contact is an observation, not a claim about what data was sent.

03 · CONTROL

Keep it local.

Your report history is encrypted on your device. Choose exports, separately consent to optional protections and advisor requests, or delete private local data.

Evidence has limits. Historical contacts do not show payloads or prove harm. Optional Safari protection covers Safari resources, and encrypted DNS sends query names to the chosen resolver. Fire Privacy cannot change another app’s permissions. This expanded build is pending native/device validation and release.
